# Tim MacDonough Painting Company — Brand Guidelines

---

## Brand Positioning

**Category:** Luxury Residential & High-End Commercial Painting
**Tagline:** *Elevated Service. Timeless Results.*
**Secondary Tagline:** Craftsmanship for Distinguished Homes
**Brand Promise:** Rooted in Trust. Delivering Excellence.
**Established:** 2006

Tim MacDonough Painting Company is not a general painting contractor.
It is a **white-glove painting service** for discerning homeowners and
property owners who expect precision, professionalism, and results that
reflect the value of their investment.

## Target Demographic

- **Household income:** $200,000+ per year
- **Homeowner profile:** High-end residential — custom builds, luxury
  renovations, historic estates, architectural statement homes
- **Geography:** Metro Detroit — Rochester Hills, Bloomfield Hills,
  Lake Orion, Oxford, Birmingham, Troy, and Oakland/Macomb County
- **Mindset:** They are not price-shopping. They are quality-shopping.
  They want a contractor who respects their home, communicates clearly,
  and delivers a result that matches the caliber of the property.
- **Decision drivers:** Reputation, referrals, visual proof of quality,
  and a brand that looks as premium as the homes they own.

| Elevated Taupe   | `#A79B8B` | Muted text, secondary labels, dividers             |
| Stone Gray       | `#6E7277` | Supporting text, captions, placeholder text        |

### Color Rules
- **Never** use Heritage Navy and a dark charcoal together in body text —
  reserve navy for display/headline use or dark section backgrounds.
- Signature Gold is an **accent only** — never use as a large background
  fill on its own.
- Warm Cream is the preferred page background (not pure white) to
  reinforce the warm, elevated feel.
- On dark (navy) backgrounds: use Warm Cream or white for text,
  Signature Gold for accents.

## Typography

### Primary Serif — Cormorant Garamond
- **Use for:** Headlines, hero titles, section headings, pull quotes,
  any display text
- **Weight:** 400 (regular) for elegance, 700 (bold) for impact
- **Feel:** Heritage, luxury, timeless — evokes fine craftsmanship
- **Google Fonts import name:** `Cormorant+Garamond`

### Supporting Sans-Serif — Montserrat
- **Use for:** Body copy, navigation, labels, buttons, captions,
  form fields, utility text
- **Weights:** 400 (body), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold)
- **Feel:** Clean, modern, trustworthy — pairs with Cormorant to balance
  elegance with clarity
- **Google Fonts import name:** `Montserrat`

### Type Scale (desktop)
| Role              | Font              | Size      | Weight |
|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| Hero Headline     | Cormorant Garamond| 3.5–5rem  | 700    |
| Section Heading   | Cormorant Garamond| 2.2–3rem  | 700    |
| Sub-heading       | Cormorant Garamond| 1.4–1.8rem| 400    |
| Section Label     | Montserrat        | 0.75rem   | 700    |
| Body Text         | Montserrat        | 1rem      | 400    |
| Button / CTA      | Montserrat        | 0.9rem    | 600    |
| Caption / Fine    | Montserrat        | 0.78rem   | 400    |

### Typography Rules
- Section labels (e.g. "OUR SERVICES") are always Montserrat, uppercase,
  wide letter-spacing (0.15em+), Signature Gold color.
- Never set headlines in Montserrat — Cormorant Garamond is the display
  voice of the brand.
- Line height for body: 1.7. For headlines: 1.1–1.2.

## Brand Voice & Copy Tone

### Personality
- **Refined** — speaks like a trusted expert, not a salesperson
- **Confident** — earns trust through specificity and proof
- **Warm** — approachable luxury, not cold or intimidating
- **Local** — rooted in Metro Detroit, proud of the community

### Tone Descriptors
Authoritative · Elegant · Personal · Detail-oriented · Trustworthy

### Copy Rules
1. **Lead with outcome, not process** — "A flawless finish that elevates
   your home" not "We use two coats of primer."
2. **Be specific and local** — Name neighborhoods. Name materials.
   Name credentials. Vague claims read as low-end.
3. **Speak to the homeowner's pride** — They've invested in a beautiful
   home. Our copy should reflect that we understand that.
4. **CTAs are invitations, not commands** — "Request Your Complimentary
   Estimate" not just "Click Here."
5. **Avoid discount language** — Never lead with price, deals, or
   comparisons. The client is choosing quality, not lowest bid.

### Sample Approved Copy
- *"Your home deserves more than a coat of paint — it deserves
  craftsmanship."*
- *"Trusted by the most discerning homeowners across Metro Detroit
  for over 30 years."*
- *"We don't cut corners on the homes that were built without them."*
- *"Bloomfield Hills homes have a standard. So do we."*
- *"White-glove service from the first consultation to the final
  walkthrough."*

### Words to Use
- Craftsmanship · Distinguished · Elevated · Refined · Flawless ·
  Precision · Estate · Heritage · White-glove · Timeless · Meticulous

### Words to Avoid
- Cheap · Affordable · Budget · Deal · Quick · Fast (alone — say
  "efficient" instead) · Best (without proof)

## Layout & Design Rules

- **Max content width:** 1200px
- **Section padding:** 96px top/bottom desktop, 64px mobile
- **Container side padding:** 24px
- **Card border-radius:** 4px–8px (understated elegance — avoid
  oversized rounded corners which feel consumer/app-like)
- **Borders:** Use 1px Heritage Navy or Signature Gold rule lines as
  section dividers and decorative accents — never heavy borders
- **Spacing philosophy:** Generous white space (Warm Cream space).
  Luxury brands breathe. Don't crowd.
- **Shadows:** Subtle only — `0 2px 12px rgba(14,29,58,0.08)`
- **Photography:** Real high-end homes, dramatic before/afters,
  close-up detail shots of trim and finishes. No stock clip art.
