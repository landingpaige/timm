Exterior Painting

Exteriors Built
to Last Michigan Winters.

Michigan's freeze-thaw cycles, humidity, and harsh UV are relentless on exterior paint. Tim uses premium-grade coatings specifically designed to withstand our climate — ensuring your home looks sharp and stays protected for years, not just one season.

Every exterior project starts with thorough power washing, surface preparation, caulking around windows and trim, and careful priming. We don't skip steps because your home's protection depends on each one.

  • Siding painting — wood, vinyl, fiber cement, and more
  • Trim, fascia, soffit & shutters
  • Doors & windows — precision detail work
  • Deck & fence staining or painting
  • Power washing & surface prep
  • Caulking, sealing & weatherproofing
  • Full exterior color consultations
